# ⛏️ MinELE 2026 | Numbers, Standards & Parameters Reviewer

### Philippine Mining Engineering Licensure Examination 2026

An interactive, searchable, and comprehensive study resource designed to help aspiring Mining Engineers review and memorize important **numerical requirements, regulatory standards, technical parameters, and legal provisions** relevant to the Philippine Mining Engineering Licensure Examination (MinELE) 2026.

The reviewer consolidates selected numerical provisions from Philippine mining laws, environmental regulations, and mine safety standards into an organized, easy-to-navigate online reference.

## 📚 Reviewer Coverage

| Category | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Mining Laws | RA 7942, DAO 2010-21, RA 12253 |
| Allied Mining Laws | RA 7076, RA 8371, RA 4274, related amendments and executive orders |
| Water Quality | RA 9275, DAO 2016-08, DAO 2021-19 |
| Air Quality | RA 8749, DAO 2000-81 |
| Mine Safety & Health | DAO 2000-98 |
| Waste Management | RA 9003, RA 6969, DAO 2013-22 |
| EIA & Mine Rehabilitation | PD 1586 and applicable mining regulations |

The reviewer emphasizes permit durations, maximum areas, percentages, environmental limits, compliance deadlines, penalties, mine safety requirements, and other important numerical parameters.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Interactive Study Tables** — Organized numerical reference tables.
- **Water Quality Matrices** — Freshwater and marine classifications, Water Quality Guidelines (WQG), and General Effluent Standards (GES).
- **Air Quality Tables** — Pollutant limits and averaging periods.
- **Mine Safety Standards** — Numerical exposure limits and safety requirements.
- **Quick Recall Mode** — Hide numerical answers to test memorization.
- **Comparison Tables** — Compare related limits and requirements.
- **Master Search** — Search recorded regulations, values, and parameters.
- **Bookmarks** — Save selected entries for future review.
- **Dark Mode** — Comfortable studying in different lighting conditions.
- **Mobile-Friendly Layout** — Accessible on desktops, tablets, and smartphones.
- **Offline Access** — The HTML reviewer can be opened locally.

## 🚀 Access the Reviewer

**[Open the MinELE 2026 Numerical Reviewer](https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/MinELE-2026-Numerical-Reviewer/)**

Replace `YOUR-USERNAME` with your actual GitHub username after enabling GitHub Pages.

## 💻 Running Locally

1. Download the `index.html` file from this repository.
2. Open it using any modern web browser.
3. Select a reviewer category.
4. Use the search, filtering, comparison, or recall features to study.

No installation, login, or server is required.

## 🧠 Recommended Study Method

**Study → Understand → Memorize → Recall → Compare**

First, understand the applicability of each numerical standard. Then use Quick Recall to test your memory and Comparison Tables to distinguish similar requirements across different laws.

Focus on both the numerical value and the conditions under which it applies.

## ⚖️ Accuracy and Disclaimer

This project is an **independent educational reviewer** and is not an official publication of the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB), or Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR).

Selected numerical values have been checked against published legal and regulatory sources. However, not all entries have undergone complete amendment-by-amendment verification.

Regulations may be amended, superseded, or subject to transitional provisions. Readers should consult official government publications when confirming current legal requirements.

**This reviewer is intended for educational and examination preparation purposes, not as a substitute for an official regulatory compliance reference.**
