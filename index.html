Water classification & numerical standards

Classes run vertically, parameters horizontally—matching your handwritten review table. Use the matrix selector for receiving-water WQG versus discharge GES. Scroll horizontally on smaller screens.

Exam reminder: WQG = conditions in a receiving water body; GES = discharge standards. NDA means no discharge allowed; — means the cited table provides no numerical guideline. Numeric limits are not interchangeable between WQG and GES.

Board exam memory: A = team, nurse per 250/shift, doctor each shift, FT dentist, hospital; B = team, FT nurse + doctor, PT dentist, clinic; C = aider, FT nurse, PT doctor + dentist, clinic; D = FT aider, PT nurse + doctor + dentist, treatment room.

Rule 864 time definitions: Part-time health personnel render at least 4 hours/day; full-time health personnel at least 8 hours/day. Rule 865: conditional exception to establishing an emergency hospital and/or dental clinic where a hospital or dental clinic is within 5 km or reachable within 25 minutes; check exact conditions in the Order.

Board exam note: The source uses “not less than” and “not more than,” producing overlaps at exact boundary values (UG: 25, 50, 150; surface: 50, 75, 250). The ranges above reproduce the DAO wording, rather than inventing exclusive cutoffs. For actual classification at a boundary, consult MGB.
